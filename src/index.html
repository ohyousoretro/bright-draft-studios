What We Do

Websites built to perform, not just to look pretty.

At Bright Draft Studio, we primarily work with local service based businesses: plumbers, roofers, accountants, and other trades, but we also have worked with creatives as well. But we don't just build it and disappear. Your site comes with ongoing hosting, support, and monthly edits built right into one flat rate, no surprise invoices, no "that'll be extra," no chasing us down when something needs to change. As your business grows, your site grows with it.
